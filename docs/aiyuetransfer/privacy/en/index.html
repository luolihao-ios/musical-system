Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 8, 2026

No data is collected

aiyuetransfer does not require an account and does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not upload your personal information, file contents, or usage records to developer servers.

Local network and files

The app uses local-network access to transfer files through a computer browser on the same Wi-Fi network. Only files you choose are transferred; they remain on your devices and authorized local devices.

Diagnostic logs

Diagnostic logs remain on your device and are never sent automatically. We receive them only if you choose to submit them for support.
